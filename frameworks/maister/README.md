# Maister PSF Framework — Cheat Sheet

Operational framing for B2BForce skills + workspace. Not a book summary.

Source: David Maister, [*Managing the Professional Service Firm*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Managing_the_Professional_Service_Firm) (1997).

## What is a professional service firm (PSF)?

- Sells **expertise and judgment of people**, not physical products.
- Revenue from **projects, retainers, and engagements** — not product licenses or units shipped.
- Success depends on **attracting clients**, **delivering quality work**, and **developing talent**.
- Examples: digital agencies, software consultancies, management consulting, law firms, accounting firms, architecture studios, executive search firms, IT services providers.

**Not PSFs:** e-commerce stores, product SaaS (where the product is software, not consulting), manufacturing, retail.

## Agent guidance

When unsure where to put output, ask:

1. Is this about **our firm** → `workspace/firm/`
2. Is this **market-facing expertise** → `workspace/marketing/`
3. Is this **winning or managing a client relationship** → `workspace/sales/`
4. Is this **watching the market** → `workspace/intelligence/`

Always read `workspace/firm/profile.md` first.
