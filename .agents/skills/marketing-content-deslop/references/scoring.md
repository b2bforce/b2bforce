# Scoring 5 × 10

Five dimensions, each 1–10. Threshold: **35/50**. Dimensions after stop-slop (MIT);
the anchors are our own. The score describes the text, not the number of findings:
one strong punchline in a short text can cost more than three weak words in a long one.

| Dimension | Question | 3 | 6 | 9 |
|-----------|----------|---|---|---|
| Directness | Does it state, or announce? | Every other paragraph opens with an announcement or a rhetorical question | Occasional run-ups | Every paragraph opens with content |
| Rhythm | Varied or metronomic? | Even sentences and paragraphs, punchlines closing sections | Even in places | Sentence and paragraph length follow the content |
| Trust in the reader | Does it respect the reader's intelligence? | Explains the obvious, repeats itself, hedges every claim | A few repetitions | Says it once and moves on |
| Authenticity | Does it sound like a specific person or firm? | No first person, no detail, words from the overused lists | Voice only at the start | Own observations and detail throughout, in the firm's voice |
| Density | Can anything be cut? | 30%+ of sentences can go without loss | 10–20% | Almost nothing to cut |

Rules:

- Record a number per dimension with one sentence of justification.
- A total of 35+ with any `strong` pattern is still `revise`.
- The firm's voice shifts the anchors: if it writes in short sentences, short
  sentences do not lower Rhythm.
