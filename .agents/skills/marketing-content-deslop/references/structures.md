# Structural patterns (all languages)

Visible only at paragraph or whole-text level, so check them on the first full read,
before the sentence lists. Each has an `ST-` ID and a strength like the language lists.

| ID | Pattern | How to spot it | Strength | Instead |
|----|---------|----------------|----------|---------|
| ST-01 | Metronome | Three or more sentences in a row of similar length (±3 words), across several paragraphs | weak | Merge two sentences or split one |
| ST-02 | Even paragraphs | Nearly every paragraph has 3–4 sentences | weak | Paragraph length follows the thought |
| ST-03 | Punchline per section | The last sentence of 2+ sections is a short maxim restating the point | strong | End the section on a fact or a transition |
| ST-04 | Personality only at the edges | Anecdote in the intro, impersonal middle, CTA at the end | strong | The firm's voice and observations inside the body |
| ST-05 | "Challenges and outlook" | "Despite the challenges… the future…" with no new facts | strong | Cut, or one concrete challenge with an example |
| ST-06 | Summary that repeats the text | The last section restates the earlier ones | strong | The reader's next step |
| ST-07 | Symmetric sections | Every section has the same skeleton (definition → benefits → example → punchline) | weak | Structure follows the reader's questions |
| ST-08 | Text about the text | "In this section we will…", "As mentioned above", describing where data came from instead of giving it | strong | Delete the meta-commentary |
| ST-09 | Lists instead of argument | Most of the text is bullets with no connecting sentences | weak | Prose; lists only for steps and comparisons |
| ST-10 | Empty headings | "Introduction", "Benefits", "Summary" | weak | The heading says what the section shows |
| ST-11 | Same sentence openings | 3+ consecutive sentences start with the same subject or word | weak | Change the subject or merge |
| ST-12 | Writing to the wrong reader | Explaining basics the persona knows (telling an agency owner what a client is) | weak | Cut; keep what they do not know |
