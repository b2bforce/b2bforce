are inspired by:

| Source | What we took | License |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) (v3.1.0, `225a6f3`) | Pattern groups (staging, rhythm, inflation, formatting, chat residue, wrong reader), "add no facts", the before/after fact check, the "when not to act" section, strong vs weak patterns | MIT, © 2025 Siqi Chen |

| [getsentry/skills `blog-writing-guide`](https://github.com/getsentry/skills) (`f57ada2`) | The banned-word list idea and "personality only at the edges" (ST-04, EN-F06) | Apache 2.0 |
| [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) | Background for the English list | CC BY-SA 4.0 (no text copied) |

The Polish list (`patterns-pl.md`) is our own, covering nominalizations, calques
from English, and Polish typography. No list is carried over verbatim.

The MIT licenses require keeping the copyright notice when substantial portions are
copied. Nothing is copied, so this attribution is sufficient.
