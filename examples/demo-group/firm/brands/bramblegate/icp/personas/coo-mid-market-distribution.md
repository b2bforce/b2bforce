# Persona — COO, mid-market distributor

## Who they are

Runs warehouse, purchasing, and customer operations. Ten-plus years at the
company; the workarounds are theirs, which makes them both the buyer and the
person whose spreadsheet this project retires.

## What they judge success by

Whether ordering happens against the system number without a phone call, and
whether month end stops requiring their personal weekend.

## How they buy

Reactive and reference-driven. Will ask to speak to another distributor before
signing. Reads the out-of-scope list first.

## Objections in their words

- "Our data is a mess — you will drown in it."
- "The ERP partner quoted us half of this."
- "Who maintains the mappings when you leave?"

## What convinces them

A named engagement at a comparable distributor, the handover mechanism stated
concretely, and a refusal to promise a data-quality percentage.
