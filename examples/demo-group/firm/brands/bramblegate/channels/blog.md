# Channel — Bramblegate blog

## What to publish here

One long-form piece a month answering a buyer question from a real discovery
call, problem or education stage. The blog is the reference the LinkedIn posts
point at, not a separate stream.

## How publishing happens

Draft validated, human tone review, pasted into the CMS by whoever reviewed it.
The idea file moves to `status: generated` only after the post is live.

## Rhythm

First Tuesday of the month. One slot; an unfilled slot rolls over rather than
being filled with filler.
