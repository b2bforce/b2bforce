# Channel — Bramblegate LinkedIn

## What to publish here

Operational problem-stage posts for the COO persona: stock mismatches, invoice
lag, the phone-call-before-big-orders pattern. One mechanism per post. Nothing
about finance or reporting — that vocabulary belongs to the sibling brand's
channels, and mixing them is how a brand stops meaning anything.

## How publishing happens

Rhea queues the week's approved drafts in Buffer on Friday for the following
week. Buffer owns the clock. Replies to comments are native and same-day.

## Rhythm

Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 08:30. Three a week is deliberate — the brand is
in build phase and consistency beats volume.
