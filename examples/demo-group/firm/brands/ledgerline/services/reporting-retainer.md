# Monthly finance-reporting retainer

## Main problem

A CFO whose management pack arrives on working day twelve, assembled by hand
from exports, and disagrees with what operations reported a week earlier.

## Job to be done

A monthly management pack the CFO can defend, produced from the operational data
on a fixed working-day schedule, maintained as a retainer.

## Expected outcomes

- The pack lands on the same working day every month.
- Finance and operations quote the same stock and margin numbers.
- Changes to the pack are requested, versioned, and delivered inside the
  retainer instead of becoming spreadsheet forks.

We prepare management reporting, not audited statements — statutory compliance
stays with the client's accountants.

## Process

1. Inventory the current pack and every manual step behind it.
2. Rebuild the pack on the ERP data, one section per month.
3. Fixed monthly cadence: refresh, review call, change queue.

## Deliverables

Versioned pack definition in the client's repository, monthly refresh, one
review call per month, a change queue with agreed turnaround.

## Fit criteria

- ERP data already trustworthy — typically after an integration engagement.
- A finance team of at least 2 that will own the numbers in front of the board.
- The CFO attends the monthly review call.

## Anti-fit criteria

- Operational data still disputed between systems — fix that first, and that is
  a different brand's project.
- The client wants outsourced bookkeeping.
