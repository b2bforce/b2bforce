# Channel — Ledgerline newsletter

## What to publish here

One idea per issue, written for the CFO persona in monthly-close vocabulary:
what a defensible management pack looks like, where packs silently fork, what
"working day five" costs to reach. Issues repurpose the month's best blog
material compressed to one takeaway — the newsletter is a digest with an
opinion, not a link dump.

Never here: client names or figures outside `firm/proof/`, and nothing that
reads as audit or statutory advice.

## How publishing happens

Drafted from the month's content, reviewed by Rhea, sent through the mailing
tool to the opt-in list. The send is manual on purpose: a newsletter that
nobody re-reads before sending is the channel's documented failure mode.

## Rhythm

Last Friday of the month, 10:00. One issue, every month, even a short one —
a skipped month costs more trust than a thin one.
