# Persona — CFO, mid-market distributor

## Who they are

Owns the close, the board pack, and a finance team of two to eight. Inherited
the reporting spreadsheet from a predecessor and knows exactly which cells are
load-bearing.

## What they judge success by

The pack landing on the same working day every month, and never again being
corrected by operations in front of the board.

## How they buy

Considered. Compares against a hire, asks for the pack definition to be owned in
the company's own repository, and wants the exit path stated before the start.

## Objections in their words

- "What happens to the pack if we stop paying you?"
- "Why a retainer and not a one-off build?"
- "Can you work from the numbers operations already trusts?"

## What convinces them

A fixed working-day schedule, versioned pack definitions they own, and evidence
from the same company's operational side that the underlying data holds.
