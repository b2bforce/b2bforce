# ICP — Distribution finance teams (Ledgerline)

## Segment

Finance teams at EU distributors whose operational data is already consistent —
often after an integration project — but whose management pack is still
assembled by hand from exports every month.

## Why they buy

`buying_mode: considered`. The pain is chronic rather than acute: the pack is
late every month, not catastrophically once. They compare against hiring an
analyst and against doing nothing.

## Pain points

| Pain | Consequence | Current workaround |
|------|-------------|--------------------|
| Pack built by hand from exports | Working day twelve, every month | Overtime in close week |
| Finance and ops numbers differ | Board time spent reconciling, not deciding | Pre-meeting alignment calls |
| Pack changes fork the spreadsheet | Nobody trusts which version is current | "Final_v3_REAL" file names |

## Trigger events

- A board asks why finance and operations quoted different margins.
- The analyst who builds the pack hands in notice.
- An integration project makes the underlying data trustworthy for the first
  time.

## Objections

- "We should hire instead." A fair comparison, and the one to argue honestly.
- "Our accountants do this." They do statutory work; this is the management pack.

## Anti-fit signals

Operational data still disputed between systems, or the buyer wants outsourced
bookkeeping.
