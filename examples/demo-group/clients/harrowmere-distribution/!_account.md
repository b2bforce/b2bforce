# Harrowmere Distribution

## What we do for them

One account, two brands — which is the point of this demo. Bramblegate
delivered the ERP integration (January–April) and Ledgerline now runs the
monthly reporting retainer sold on the back of it. `brands` lists both;
`services` uses the qualified `{brand}/{slug}` form because this file is shared
and a bare slug would be ambiguous.

## What the relationship depends on

Two different economic buyers in one company: Nils judges the group on whether
operations runs without phone-call reconciliation, Petra on whether the pack
lands on its working day. A QBR that only speaks to one of them is half a QBR.

## Open risks

- The retainer is young; the honest health check is whether the pack lands on
  schedule for a full quarter.
- Petra compares the retainer against an internal hire every renewal — the exit
  path promise has to stay true, or the comparison flips.
