# Discovery — Harrowmere reporting retainer

## Situation

Harrowmere Distribution is a Bramblegate client: the ERP integration closed in
April and their stock and invoicing numbers have been consistent since. In the
April close review, CFO Petra Innes asked whether "the people who fixed the
stock number" could also fix the management pack. This opportunity belongs to
**Ledgerline** — the group's retainer brand — and sits in that brand's segment,
while the client account stays shared. That is the multi-brand mechanism this
demo exists to show.

One call, 9 June, with Petra and Nils Fairweather (COO).

## Problem in the buyer's words

> "The pack lands on working day twelve, and the first ten minutes of every
> board meeting are spent arguing about whose margin number is right."

## Impact

Stated by the buyer, not estimated by us: close week costs the finance team
overtime every month, and the board has twice deferred a pricing decision
because finance and operations brought different numbers.

## Desired outcome

A management pack on a fixed working day, from the ERP data operations already
trusts, owned in Harrowmere's own repository.

## Decision process

| Role | Name | Part in the decision |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Economic buyer | Petra Innes (CFO) | Holds the budget, compares against hiring an analyst |
| User | Finance team of 4 | Build the pack by hand today |
| Influencer | Nils Fairweather (COO) | Sponsor of the integration work; trusts the group |

## Constraints

- Petra wants the first rebuilt pack section before the September board meeting.
- The comparison is an internal hire; the retainer must beat it on start date
  and exit path, not on price.

## Firm assessment

Maps to `reporting-retainer`, good fit: the operational data is trustworthy
(the anti-fit that usually kills this service is already resolved), the finance
team will own the numbers, and Petra attends the review call herself.

`brief_quality: workable` — one call, and the open questions below are real.

## Open questions

2. Who on the finance team owns the pack definition after handover?
