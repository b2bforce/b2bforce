## Situation

The management pack lands on working day twelve, assembled by hand from
exports, and the first minutes of each board meeting are spent reconciling
finance's numbers with operations'. Petra put it that way on 9 June, and the
constraint behind it is simple: the pack is rebuilt manually every month from
data that is now, since April, actually consistent underneath.

## Outcomes

When this retainer is running, these should be true:

- The pack lands on the same working day every month, without close-week
  overtime being the mechanism.
- Finance and operations quote the same stock and margin numbers, because both
  read from the ERP rather than from exports of exports.
- Changes to the pack go through a versioned change queue instead of forking
  the spreadsheet.

We prepare management reporting, not audited statements. Statutory work stays
with your accountants, and we will say so again whenever the two are at risk of
being confused.

## Approach

Month one inventories the current pack and every manual step behind it. From
month two we rebuild it on the ERP data, one section per month, board-read
sections first — the answer to the open question about which sections the board
actually reads decides that order. Each month runs on a fixed cadence: refresh,
one review call with Petra, and the change queue.

## Scope

1. Inventory of the current pack and its manual steps.
2. Versioned pack definition in your repository.
3. Monthly refresh on the agreed working day.
4. One review call per month with the CFO.
5. Change queue with agreed turnaround inside the retainer.

## Out of scope

- Statutory or audit reporting of any kind.
- Bookkeeping, postings, or corrections in the ERP.
- New data integrations — if one is needed, that is a separate conversation
  with the group's project brand, priced separately.
- Ad-hoc analysis outside the pack, beyond the change queue's turnaround.

## Assumptions

- The ERP data stays the trusted source it is today; if the underlying
  integrations change, the pack definition is re-baselined as a change.
- Someone on the finance team is named owner of the pack definition.
- Petra attends the monthly review call, or delegates it explicitly.

## Change control

Anything outside Scope is a change: estimated in writing inside the change
queue, approved in writing, then scheduled. Nothing outside Scope appears on an
invoice you have not seen first.
