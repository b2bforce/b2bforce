# Outcome — Northwind warehouse cutover

## What happened

- 11 and 18 May — two discovery calls with Anna and Piotr.
- 20 May — proposal sent, full engagement recommended at 34,000 EUR.
- 26 May — call confirmed the parallel-running question: the legacy integration is
  read-only after cutover, so the assumption held and scope did not change.
- 15 June — Anna accepted the full engagement under the existing master agreement.
  Start 8 June was held.

## Reason the buyer gave

> "Same team, and you already know our pipeline. I am not spending September
> explaining our release process to somebody new."

## Our assessment

`reason_assessed` is `unknown` on purpose. The field's vocabulary describes why deals
are **lost**, and there is no honest value in it for this win. What we can say without
inventing a category: there was no competitor in the process, the master agreement
removed procurement, and the September leave date made switching vendors expensive for
them rather than for us.

Worth noticing rather than celebrating: this was won on a relationship built by
delivered work, which is the channel the firm profile says produces most of its
engagements. That claim now has one more data point behind it.

## What we would do differently

Ask about the provider's test environment on the first call, not the second. It was
the only assumption that could have moved the date, and we spent a week not knowing
the answer.
