# Firm Profile — Ferrymark Systems

A fictional 14-person platform delivery consultancy, used as the demo firm for this
repo. See [examples/README.md](../../README.md) before reading further.

## What we do

We take mid-market logistics platforms off manual release processes. Two engagement
shapes: a fixed-scope platform migration project, and a smaller support retainer for
teams that want the pipeline maintained after handover.

We sell one service in this demo — `platform-migration` — because one service is
enough to show how the workflows chain.

## Positioning

We are the firm you call when releases have become a weekend activity and the two
people who understand the process have started getting recruiter calls. We compete
against staff augmentation shops on the same budget and win on the handover: our
engagements end with the client's own engineers running the pipeline.

We do not take on greenfield product builds, and we do not do staff augmentation by
the seat.

## Who we sell to

Mid-market logistics and freight operators in the EU, 200–800 employees, with an
in-house engineering team of 8 to 40. Primary ICP: `mid-market-logistics`.

## How work reaches us

Referrals from delivered engagements first, then inbound from the blog, then
conference conversations. Outbound is a small share and we treat it as the weakest
channel rather than the default one.

## Delivery model

A principal is on every engagement four days a week for its duration. No junior
staffing behind a senior name, and no handoff to a second team after signature.
This constrains how many engagements run in parallel, which is a deliberate trade.

## Language and tone

English. Plain, specific, and willing to say what an approach does not cover. No
superlatives about ourselves, and no client numbers that are not in
`firm/proof/`.
