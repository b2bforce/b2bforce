# Idea

The buyer has CI, believes that means releases are automated, and still loses a
weekend a month. The gap is that CI runs tests while a human still decides whether
the release is safe. Naming that distinction is the whole value of the post.

## Why this idea

Every discovery call in this segment includes some version of "we already have
GitHub Actions". Answering it in public saves the first ten minutes of the call and
qualifies out teams whose problem is something else.
