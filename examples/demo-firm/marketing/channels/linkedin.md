# Channel — Ferrymark LinkedIn

## What to publish here

Short problem-stage observations from delivery work, written for the CTO
persona — one concrete mechanism per post, no listicles. Repurposing a blog
post is fine when it compresses to one idea; linking the blog in the first
comment, not the body.

## How publishing happens

Approved drafts are queued in Buffer on Monday for the week. Buffer owns the
clock; nobody posts natively except to reply to comments. A post that misses
review by Monday waits for the next slot — the queue is never fed same-day.

## Rhythm

Tuesday and Thursday, 09:00. Two posts a week is the ceiling; the feed goes
quiet before it goes filler.
